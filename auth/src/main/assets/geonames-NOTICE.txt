Contains GeoNames data reduced to country code, administrative region name,
and populated place name. Snapshot retrieved 2026-11-12. The city catalogue
covers cities15000 (population over 15,000 or capitals), not every municipality.
Names are suggestions, not a jurisdiction validation or territorial assertion.
Manual entry remains available for missing, historical or alternative names.
Reproduce using scripts/geography/build_catalogue.py with the two source files.
